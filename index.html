Why 3D

Visual learning increases retention.

That's the reason every one of these apps exists. You remember what you've seen, and you remember it better when you've turned it over and changed it yourself. So each app is the same tool pointed at a different subject: a 3D model you can see, work and take apart.

One

See it

Real geometry from any angle. Orbit it, zoom in, look behind it.

Two

Work it

Change a value, add a part, run it. Watch what happens and why.

Three

Take it apart

Explode the model and see every piece and where it goes.

The apps

Illuminate electricity.

Build circuits in 3D and watch the current move. The W.I.R.E. engine works out watts, current, resistance and voltage as you build, and F.U.S.E. shows which part fails first and why.

  1. 01 · BUILD

    Build it and watch it run.

    Lay out a circuit on the bench and W.I.R.E. works out the numbers at every part while you build. The current moves along the wires, so you can follow where it goes.

  2. 02 · ARENA

    Put it in the Arena.

    The Component Arena runs parts against their real ratings under different conditions: lab bench, arctic cold, engine bay, desert. Then it ranks how they held up.

  3. 03 · OVERLOAD

    Push it past its rating.

    Run a part harder than it's rated for and the readout says by how much. That capacitor is sitting at 4,267% of its rated watts.

  4. 04 · F.U.S.E.

    See why it failed.

    F.U.S.E. shows the failure as it happens and which part let go first. You see the reason, not just the result, and that's the part you remember.

2D plan in. 3D framing out.

Put a floor plan underneath, trace it and frame it in wood, steel or engineered lumber. Then explode the model and see every stud, plate and header.

  1. 01 · PLAN

    Put the plan underneath.

    Bring in a floor plan and trace over it. Floors, framing, roof, plumbing, electrical and HVAC each get their own step, in the order a build goes.

  2. 02 · STAND IT UP

    Stand it up.

    The walls you traced become a framed building you can orbit. Tap a wall to change it, or add doors, windows and fixtures.

  3. 03 · EXPLODE

    Explode it.

    Pull the floors apart and every joist, stud, plate and header is right there, where it goes.

  4. 04 · COUNT

    Count it.

    The material takeoff keeps count as you go: feet of wall framing, studs at 16 inches on centre, plate stock and sheathing sheets.

Who builds them

Built by a tradesperson who wanted to know how it works.

I'm Mitchell Lorin McKnight, a carpenter in Surrey, BC. In first-year electrical the circuit math was hard, and the teacher went fast. I went looking for a 3D circuit simulator to help me see it. Nobody had built one, so I built it.

Every app since has started the same way: something I wanted to see inside that nobody had made for ordinary people. If you can't get to it, can't afford it or can't safely take it apart, these apps let you do it in 3D.
