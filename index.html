F.U.S.E.™

Failure Understanding Simulation Engine

Knowing that a part fails is useful. Knowing why it failed is what sticks. F.U.S.E. works from real component specifications, so when a fuse opens, a joist deflects or a bearing seizes, the cause is shown, not just the result.

It was first built for CircuiTry3D to show what a fuse actually does. It now runs underneath every app in the family.

03

About

Built by a tradesperson, for the people learning the trade.

Mitchell Lorin McKnight is a carpenter and electrical apprentice based in Surrey, British Columbia. In his first year of electrical school he struggled to picture what the circuit theory described. He wanted something he could turn over and look at from every side. He couldn't find one, so he built it.

CircuiTry3D launched on Google Play in March 2026. ThePrints3D applied the same approach to framing, and AutoMotive3D to engines.
